The Sourdough Process

Twenty-four hour slow fermentation.

Time is our primary ingredient. We rest our dough for a full day and night, allowing the natural wild yeast starter to break down the starches. This slow process develops a complex, sour flavor profile and creates a crumb that is light, open, and easy to digest.

Stoneground Grain
Our Ingredients

Single-origin flour & pure sea salt.

We partner with local dry-land grain farmers who mill our flour stoneground, preserving the nutrient-dense germ and bran. Combined with unrefined sea salt and filtered spring water, we bake a loaf that tastes deeply of the soil and sunshine of the Pacific Northwest.
