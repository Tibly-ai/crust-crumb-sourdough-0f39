The Daily Bake

Every single loaf we sell begins with three simple ingredients: regional organic flour, filtered mountain water, and sea salt. Guided by wild yeast and shaped by hand, our dough rests for a patient twenty four hours before entering our wood-fired stone oven at sunrise.
